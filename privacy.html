Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

English

"my piece" (the Shop, https://stlogisapo.base.shop/) uses its own application to publish the Shop's own product videos to the Shop's own TikTok account. The application only posts content created by the Shop. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information of TikTok users or visitors.

The only data handled is the authorization token of the Shop's own TikTok account, used solely to publish the Shop's videos. For customer personal information handled when purchasing, please see the Shop's privacy policy: https://stlogisapo.base.shop/privacy
